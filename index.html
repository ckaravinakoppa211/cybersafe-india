🛡️ Quick Safety Rules

  • Never share your OTP or UPI PIN.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Verify unexpected payment requests.
  • Never share your password.
  • Use official websites and phone numbers.

🚨 Need Help?

If you think you have experienced cyber financial fraud in India:

  1. Contact your bank immediately.
  2. Save messages and transaction details.
  3. Call 1930 for cyber financial fraud.
  4. Report the incident through the official Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
Remember:
Never give your OTP, UPI PIN or password to someone who contacts you unexpectedly.
